CareWell Family Clinic

A social media strategy and 30-day content calendar for a small neighbourhood clinic starting from zero, with a small team and a modest budget.

Role
Strategy, content, community
Platforms
Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Telegram
Deliverable
17-slide strategy deck
Type
Portfolio concept, no client results claimed

The deck

CareWell strategy

Use the arrows, swipe on a phone, or press ← and →. Esc closes the expanded view.

Why these choices

Trust before volume

People pick a clinic they trust, not the one that posts most. A small team also can’t sustain daily high-production content, so the plan favours a realistic cadence of useful posts.

Five platforms, one job each

Instagram for visual stories, Facebook for events and local groups, LinkedIn for partnerships, YouTube for longer explainers, Telegram for reminders families forward to each other. Distinct jobs stop the team posting the same thing five times.

Useful first, promotional second

Education and trust posts earn attention before conversion posts ask for appointments, so the ask feels like a natural next step.

Clinical guardrails

Every tip ends with a nudge to see a qualified clinician, and clinical questions in comments go to staff. One wrong answer can undo months of trust.

A day-by-day calendar

Platform, format, hook, call to action and objective for each post let a small team batch a week at a time and keep the pillars balanced.

KPIs, not invented numbers

With no live data, the deck defines what to measure and how to test ads one variable at a time instead of showing made-up results.
